    About us

    A little interest. A sweet beginning.

    Bun Bun Cafe started with something simple: Monisha Janet's personal interest in baking and her desire to turn that interest into something of her own.

    With the encouragement and support of her family, Monisha took her first step towards building a home-baking venture. The name Bun Bun was suggested by Allwyn Jerold.

    What began as a simple interest gradually became Bun Bun Cafe, a homegrown bakery built around creativity, passion and the love of baking.

    Today, Monisha continues to develop the brand with her baking knowledge, creativity and dedication, bringing together delightful treats and personalised creations for every occasion.
