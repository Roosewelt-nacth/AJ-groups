About us

A little interest. A sweet beginning.

Bun Bun Cafe started with something simple: Monisha Janet's personal interest in baking and her desire to turn that interest into something of her own. With the encouragement and support of her family, she took her first step towards building a home-baking venture.

What began as a simple interest gradually became Bun Bun Cafe, a homegrown bakery built around creativity, passion and the love of baking. As a certified baker, Monisha leads the baking, the product preparation and the creative direction of the brand.
