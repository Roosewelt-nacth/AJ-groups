About us

A little interest. A sweet beginning.

Bun Bun Cafe started with something simple: Monisha Janet's personal interest in baking and her desire to turn that interest into something of her own.

With the encouragement and support of her family, Monisha took her first step towards building a home-baking venture. What began as a simple interest gradually became Bun Bun Cafe, a homegrown bakery built around creativity, passion and the love of baking.

Today, Monisha continues to develop the brand with her baking knowledge, creativity and dedication, bringing together delightful treats and personalised creations for every occasion.

Meet our founder

Monisha Janet

Founder & Certified Baker

Behind Bun Bun Cafe is Monisha Janet, whose personal interest in baking inspired the beginning of this journey.

As a certified baker, Monisha leads the baking process, product preparation and creative direction of the brand. Her focus is on creating delicious products, exploring new flavours and designs, and delivering a thoughtful experience with every order.
