Meet the founder

AJAllwyn JeroldFounder & Entrepreneur – AJ Groups

Allwyn began his entrepreneurial journey in 2022 with AJ Dynamics. Driven by a passion for building businesses and creating new opportunities, he is now developing AJ Groups as a parent identity for independent brands and emerging ventures.

His focus is on building strong foundations, creating distinctive brands and turning ideas into businesses with the potential to grow.
