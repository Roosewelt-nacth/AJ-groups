About us

A little interest. A sweet beginning.

Bun Bun Cafe started with something simple: Monisha Janet's personal interest in baking and her desire to turn that interest into something of her own.

With the encouragement and support of her family, Monisha took her first step towards building a home-baking venture. What began as a simple interest gradually became Bun Bun Cafe, a homegrown bakery built around creativity, passion and the love of baking.

Meet our founder

Monisha Janet

Founder & Certified Baker

As a certified baker, Monisha leads the baking process, product preparation and creative direction of the brand. Her focus is on delicious products, new flavours and designs, and a thoughtful experience with every order.
