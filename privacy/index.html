Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

AJ GROUPS (“we”, “us”) is a group of independent brands founded and led by Allwyn Jerold, based in India. This policy explains what personal information we collect on ajgroups.co and the brand pages on it, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep it as small as we can.

1. What we collect and why

  • WhatsApp chats: if you message us on WhatsApp, we receive your number and your message and use them only to reply.
  • Contact and enquiry forms (the AJ GROUPS contact form, the Alven Studio brief and the House of Fovo booking request): your name, email address, the topic you choose and your message. We use these only to reply to you.
  • Bun Bun orders: when the order system is switched on, the details you enter (name, contact details, delivery address and order notes) are sent to our order service and kept for up to 180 days, then deleted. Until then, anything you enter is stored only in your own browser.
  • Saved settings and faster loading: your light or dark theme choice, saved dates and similar preferences are kept in your browser (local storage), and a copy of the website’s own files is kept on your device so repeat visits load faster. None of this is sent to us.
  • Technical data: the services that host and deliver the site may log your IP address and browser details in order to run and secure it.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, we do not set cookies, and we do not sell or rent your information.

2. Who handles your information

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers form messages to our email inbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website, and Cloudflare manages our domain name and, for Bun Bun, the order service.
  • jsDelivr delivers a 3D graphics code library used on some pages. Some brand pages also load fonts from Google Fonts. Both can see your IP address when your browser asks for these files.
  • WhatsApp (run by Meta): if you tap “Chat on WhatsApp”, the chat opens in WhatsApp. We then see your WhatsApp number and what you write, and WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy apply to that chat.
  • Instagram and Facebook are only links. If you follow them, their own policies apply.

3. How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as we need to deal with them and for a reasonable time after. Bun Bun orders are deleted after 180 days.

4. Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Email us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time. We handle personal data in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

5. Children

This site is not designed to collect personal information from children. Our children’s brand, AJ Little Wonder World, will address parents and carers. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

6. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No online service is perfectly secure, so please do not send sensitive information such as passwords or bank details through our forms. We will never ask you for them.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date above.
